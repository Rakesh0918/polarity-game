Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Welcome to POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT (Application Package: com.games.PolarityMR.Android). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our mobile game application.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information, data collection, and device permissions.

1. Information Collection and Use

POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, create an account, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to play the game.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not request or access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, or file storage outside the application's secure private sandbox.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All game-related progression data is stored exclusively on your device using Android's sandboxed local storage:

  • Level progression, chapter unlocks, and star ratings
  • User-created puzzle grids in the Sandbox Level Editor
  • Sound, soundtrack volume, haptics, and theme preferences
  • Lifetime gameplay telemetry (moves made, undos used, fastest solve times)

This information remains entirely local to your device and is never uploaded to any remote private server.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support ongoing game development and server operations, POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT integrates Google AdMob to deliver optional rewarded video ads (e.g., granting hints, undos, or level resets) and intermittent interstitial ads:

  • Third-party ad providers may collect anonymous, non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage data (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, coarse device model, and ad interaction metrics) in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.
  • For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Services Advertising Policy.

4. Device Integrity and Security

Our application includes local on-device security checks to protect intellectual property and ensure fair gameplay:

  • The app performs local detection for rooted environments, emulators, and dynamic hooking frameworks (such as Frida or Xposed).
  • These checks execute entirely in memory on your device and do not transmit diagnostic reports to external servers.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT is designed for a general audience (recommended ages 13 and up). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. Data Deletion

Because all game data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete all game progress, preferences, and saved states at any time by clearing application data via Android Settings > Apps > POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored data.
