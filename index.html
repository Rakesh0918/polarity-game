Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 • Compliant with India's DPDP Act, 2023 & Google Play Developer Policies

Welcome to POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT (Application Package: com.games.PolarityMR.Android). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal data in accordance with global standards and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Notice & Consent (DPDP Act, 2023 — Section 5 & 6)

This Privacy Policy serves as an itemized statutory notice under Section 5 of the DPDP Act 2023 detailing what digital personal data is collected, its specified purpose, how you can exercise your Data Principal rights, and how to reach our Grievance Officer.

  • Free & Affirmative Consent: Cloud Save and Google Play Games features are entirely optional and enabled solely through clear, affirmative action when you choose to tap [Sign In with Google] or [Connect Play Games].
  • Core Game Playable Offline: You can play the full Campaign, Endless Lab, and Sandbox Level Editor without creating an account or transmitting any personal data.

2. Categories of Data Collected & Specified Purposes

A. On-Device Local Data (Non-Personal)

Stored exclusively in Android's sandboxed private storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Campaign progression, chamber unlocks, star ratings, and par moves.
  • Custom puzzle blueprints crafted in the Sandbox Level Editor.
  • Haptic, soundtrack volume, and visual studio preferences.
  • Gameplay telemetry (total moves, undos used, fastest solve times).

B. Google Account & Firebase Cloud Save (Voluntary)

When you choose to sign in to Google Cloud Save:

  • Data Processed: Email address, public display name, avatar URL (if provided), and unique Firebase Authentication User ID (UID).
  • Specified Purpose: To provide encrypted cloud backup and cross-device synchronization of your level progression and stars via Firebase Firestore.

C. Google Play Games Services v2 (Voluntary)

When you connect your Google Play Games profile:

  • Data Processed: GamerTag (e.g., DustyBug578), Google Play Games Player ID, and Magnetic Blitz high scores.
  • Specified Purpose: To populate public global Magnetic Blitz Leaderboards and calculate all-time percentile rankings.

D. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing game development and server operations, POLARITY integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads (e.g., to earn in-game hints or undos) and occasional interstitials:

  • AdMob may process anonymous device identifiers (GAID), coarse IP-derived geographic region, and ad interaction metrics strictly for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • Review Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Services Advertising Policy.

3. Children’s Personal Data (DPDP Act, 2023 — Section 9 & COPPA)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, a child is defined as an individual who has not completed 18 years of age. POLARITY does not track, profile, monitor the behavior of, or direct targeted advertisements to children. If you are a minor under 18 years of age, please obtain verifiable consent from your parent or lawful guardian before connecting your Google Account or utilizing cloud features.

4. Data Principal Statutory Rights (DPDP Act, 2023 — Sections 11–14)

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following enforceable rights:

  1. Right to Access (Section 11): Request a summary of your personal data processed and third-party identities with whom data was shared.
  2. Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Request correction of inaccurate information or complete deletion of your cloud save data.
  3. Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6(4)): Disconnect your Google Account or Play Games profile anytime directly in your in-game Player Profile.
  4. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Have your privacy concerns addressed by our designated Grievance Officer within 30 days.
  5. Right to Nominate (Section 14): Nominate an individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.

5. Data Deletion & Retention

  • Local Device Wipe: Delete all local game progress anytime via Android Settings > Apps > POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Cloud Data Erasure: To delete your Google Cloud Save account and Firebase UID permanently, email our Grievance Officer at privacy@polarity.game (or support@polarity.game) with the subject line "Data Erasure Request". All cloud records will be verified and permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Data Security & Safeguards (Section 8)

All network communications utilize industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. Cloud records are protected by declarative Firebase security rules restricting access exclusively to authenticated owners. In the event of a personal data breach, notifications will be provided to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and affected users as prescribed by law.

7. Multilingual Notice Provision (Section 5(3))

In accordance with Section 5(3) of the DPDP Act 2023, this notice is provided in English. If you require this policy in any of the 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India (such as Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, etc.), please contact our Grievance Officer and a translated copy will be furnished.
