Introduction & Our Philosophy

Welcome to Polarity: Magnetic Rift (developed by Rakesh Sharma, referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to building engaging, thoughtful puzzle games while treating player privacy with the highest respect and transparency.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, used, protected, and deleted when you download and play Polarity: Magnetic Rift on Android devices. We design our architecture around minimal data collection: you can complete every single level in the game without creating an account, sharing an email address, or providing personal information.

02

Information We Collect and How We Use It

We classify the data processed by Polarity into four distinct categories:

A. Gameplay & Progress Data (Generated Through Play)

To deliver game progression, star rewards, and puzzle state persistence, the app maintains your gameplay statistics:

  • Puzzle Progression: Cleared level IDs, 1–3 star ratings, par move counts, and fastest completion times.
  • Lifetime Telemetry: Cumulative moves made, undos used, wormhole rifts warped, prism deflections, Endless Lab chamber records, and daily challenge completion streaks.
  • In-Game Economy: Daily free hint, undo, and reset inventory counts, and optional Pro unlock status.
  • Custom Level Blueprints: Puzzles designed by you in the Level Editor or imported from QR codes and deep links.

Storage: All gameplay progression is stored locally on your device by default. It is only synchronized to secure cloud servers if you explicitly enable Google Cloud Save.

B. Google Account Information (Optional Cloud Save)

When you voluntarily link your Google Account via the in-game Player Profile > Cloud Save card, we process:

  • Your Google Account Display Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture URL.
  • A unique, randomized Firebase Authentication User ID (UID).

Purpose: We use this information exclusively to identify your cloud save slot, enabling you to restore your campaign progress, stars, and trophies across multiple devices or after reinstalling the app.

Sign Out Guarantee: You can disconnect your Google Account at any time by tapping [Sign Out] on the Google Cloud Save card. When signed out, the game immediately reverts to an anonymous local session.

C. Google Play Games Services (Optional)

If you connect to Google Play Games, the game interfaces with Google's Play Games Services v2:

  • Your public GamerTag and Player ID.
  • High scores and combo records achieved in Magnetic Blitz mode submitted to the official Google Play Blitz leaderboard (CgkIhO-OjbUUEAIQAA).

Purpose: To showcase global rankings, compare high scores with friends, and celebrate achievement milestones.

D. Device, Diagnostics & Security Data

To maintain game stability, deliver advertisements, and prevent leaderboard manipulation, the app processes standard technical signals:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Device Advertising ID (GAID), coarse geographic region (country/city level from IP), and ad delivery metrics (impressions, reward completions).
  • App Integrity (Talsec FreeRASP): On-device checks verifying the integrity of the application binary (detecting rooted environments, debuggers, or malicious hook injections) to safeguard fair leaderboard competition. This check runs entirely on your device and does not upload personal files.
03

In-Game Advertising (Google AdMob)

Polarity is free to download and play. To sustain server costs and ongoing puzzle development, we integrate Google AdMob to display advertising:

  • Rewarded Video Ads (Player-Initiated): You can voluntarily choose to watch a video ad in exchange for bonus undos, hints, or chamber resets. These ads are never forced.
  • Interstitials: Brief transition ads shown between puzzle batches, with built-in cooldowns and frequency capping.
  • Banner Ads: Standard non-intrusive display banners placed in designated interface areas.

Your Advertising Controls: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time on your Android device by going to Settings > Google > Ads and selecting "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".

For more information on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's Advertising Privacy Policies.

04

Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Polarity requests only the minimum permissions essential for gameplay features:

  • INTERNET & NETWORK STATE: Required for optional Google Cloud Save, Google Play Games leaderboards, and AdMob ads.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during magnet pulls, circuit snaps, win celebrations, and deadlock alerts. Can be toggled on/off in Audio Studio.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional / On-Demand): Requested only if you choose to import a custom level by selecting a QR code screenshot from your device gallery. The app reads only the selected QR image to decode the level blueprint.
05

Data Storage, Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your game records:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and cloud services (Firebase, Google Play, AdMob) use secure Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Strict Access Control: Cloud Firestore security rules ensure that your saved game documents can only be accessed or modified by your authenticated account.
  • Retention: Local game data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Cloud backup records are retained as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion.
06

Your Rights, Data Deletion & Account Control

We believe players must have complete authority over their personal information. You have the following direct controls:

  • Instant Sign Out: Tap [Sign Out] on the Google Cloud Save card in the Player Profile at any time to sever the cloud link immediately.
  • Local Data Reset: You can completely erase all local saves, telemetry, and custom puzzles by going to Android Settings > Apps > Polarity: Magnetic Rift > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Permanent Cloud Data Deletion: If you linked a Google Account and wish to permanently delete all cloud records, progress files, and leaderboard entries associated with your account, please send an email to rakeshsharma0918@gmail.com with the subject line "Polarity - Account & Data Erasure Request" from the email address linked to your Google Cloud Save. Your data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

Children's Privacy Protection

Polarity: Magnetic Rift is a general audience tactical puzzle game designed for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or market personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions), in strict compliance with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such records from our servers.
